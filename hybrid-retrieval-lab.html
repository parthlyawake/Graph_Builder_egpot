Decision rule

How a winner is chosen

  • Retrieval: Recall@K, MRR, nDCG and evidence precision.
  • Context: Recall under 4K / 8K / 16K token budgets and redundancy.
  • Downstream: completion accuracy and issue-resolution test pass rate.
  • Operations: indexing time, query latency, graph size and cost.
